OUR STORY

A new club with a local identity.

Saratoga Continentals FC was established in 2026 to build a competitive soccer environment centered on player development, discipline and long-term growth.

The club name connects the modern team to Saratoga's historic identity while the sporting vision looks forward: strong coaching, clear standards, meaningful competition and opportunities for players to progress.

We are building in stages. The goal is not simply to create another team, but to establish a club structure that can develop with its players, families, coaches, partners and community.

THE NAME

Why “The Continentals”?

The name gives the club a distinct Saratoga identity and a direct connection to the area's Revolutionary-era story. The purpose is not to recreate the past, but to carry forward ideas of identity, resolve and belonging into a modern football club.

OUR VALUES

How we want the club to operate.

These principles shape coaching, player development, competition and how Saratoga Continentals FC represents its community.

01

Development

Every player should have a clear path to improve technically, tactically, physically and mentally.

02

Standards

Training quality, preparation, accountability and respect matter every day — not only on match day.

03

Competition

We want players to learn how to compete with confidence, discipline and purpose.

04

Community

The club should feel connected to Saratoga and build relationships that extend beyond the field.

BUILDING THE CLUB

Grow with purpose.

SCFC is being developed in stages so the sporting structure can grow responsibly.

NOW Build the foundation

Club identity, player interest, development programs, community relationships and competitive planning.

NEXT Form competitive teams

Recruit players, organize coaching and establish the first competitive schedule.

FUTURE Expand the pathway

Grow age-group programming and create more opportunities for players to develop within the club.
