STANDARDS

Codes of Conduct

Behavior standards for everyone representing Saratoga Continentals FC.

Last updated: September 2026
ROLESPlayersParents / GuardiansCoaches / StaffVolunteersViolations

Player Code

Players must show respect for teammates, opponents, officials, coaches, facilities and spectators; follow safety instructions; avoid bullying, hazing, discriminatory conduct, threats and retaliation; and report injuries or safety concerns promptly.

Parent / Guardian Code

Parents and guardians must support a safe learning environment, avoid abusive sideline conduct, respect officials and coaches, avoid coaching from the sideline when it interferes with instruction, communicate concerns appropriately, and never pressure an injured player to continue.

Coach / Staff Code

Coaches and staff must maintain professional boundaries, follow youth-safety rules, avoid favoritism and retaliation, never use physical punishment or humiliating discipline, avoid private or secret communications with minors, follow injury and concussion protocols, and report suspected abuse or serious misconduct as required.

Volunteer Code

Volunteers must stay within their assigned role, follow supervision requirements, maintain appropriate boundaries, protect confidential information, and immediately escalate safety concerns to the appropriate authority or club contact.

Violations & Discipline

The club may restrict, suspend or remove a participant, parent, coach, staff member or volunteer from club activity for conduct inconsistent with safety, law, league rules or club policy. Serious allegations may be referred to law enforcement, child-protection authorities, governing bodies or other appropriate organizations.
