WHY PARTNER?

Support something from the beginning.

Founding partners have an opportunity to build a long-term relationship with Saratoga Continentals FC while the club's teams, development programs and community presence are taking shape.

Partnerships can be tailored around visibility, community engagement, player development, events and digital promotion. The goal is to create relationships that make sense for both the club and the businesses that support it.

PARTNER BENEFITS

Multiple ways to create value.

Benefits can be combined into tailored packages depending on the partner's goals and the stage of the club's competitive program.

01

Club Visibility

Brand presence across club digital channels, announcements and selected club materials.

02

Matchday Presence

Future visibility opportunities connected to matches, events and club activities.

03

Community Connection

Association with a growing local sports organization focused on Saratoga-area players and families.

04

Digital Promotion

Partner features, website placement and social-media opportunities as club channels develop.
