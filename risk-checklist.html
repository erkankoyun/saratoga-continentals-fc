Legal Review Priority

Before charging fees or relying on liability language, obtain New York legal review. New York General Obligations Law § 5-326 can make certain agreements that exempt owners/operators of fee-based recreational facilities from liability for their own negligence void and unenforceable. The club packet therefore uses an inherent-risk acknowledgment rather than claiming a blanket release of negligence.
