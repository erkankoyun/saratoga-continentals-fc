Saratoga Continentals FC is established

A new club identity is taking shape in Saratoga, New York, with player development, competition and community at the center of the project.

The club was established in 2026 with the goal of creating a football structure that can grow responsibly over time — from individual training and first-team competition to future youth programming and local partnerships.
