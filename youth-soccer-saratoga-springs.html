PLAYER-FIRST PATHWAY

Youth soccer built around development.

Our goal is to create a clear path from individual skill development to competitive team soccer as Saratoga Continentals FC grows.

01 Evaluate

Understand the player's age, experience, position, strengths and current development needs.

02 Develop

Build first touch, ball control, passing, dribbling, finishing, movement and decision-making.

03 Compete

Add pressure, small-sided games and realistic football situations as the player progresses.

04 Progress

Connect individual development to future age-group and competitive team opportunities.

PARENT FAQ

Before you register interest.

Clear expectations matter while the club builds its first youth development and competitive programs.

01 Are youth players being accepted?

Player-interest registration is open. Submitting the form does not guarantee team placement or confirm participation.

02 What training is planned?

The development pathway includes individual, position-specific and small-group soccer training, with competitive opportunities added as the club grows.

03 Where will training happen?

The club serves the Saratoga Springs area. Specific training or evaluation locations are provided when sessions are scheduled.

04 How are minors handled?

A parent, legal guardian or authorized adult must submit information for minor players. Review our Youth Safety information before participation.
